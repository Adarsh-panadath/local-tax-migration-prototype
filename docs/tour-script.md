# Product tour: narration script

The narration of `web/media/tour.mp4`, the film on the front page. It is spoken by **Kokoro** (the `af_heart` voice, speed 0.92), an open-source text-to-speech model (Apache 2.0) run offline, so there is no recording and no paid service. Numbers are written out in words so they are read correctly. Each scene of the film is timed to its sentences; the captions on screen say the same things in fewer words.

Length: 2:22. About 300 words, at about 158 words a minute.

| Starts at | Scene | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Opening | Moving payroll to the cloud is easy. Moving local taxes is not. |
| 0:06 | The problem | One business. A hundred and fifty-two employees. Three hundred and eighteen local tax items. Labels typed by hand, addresses missing, rates wrong. And every mistake becomes a penalty. |
| 0:20 | The product | Local Tax Migration moves every local tax from Desktop to Online payroll, and checks each one, line by line. |
| 0:29 | How it works | It works in three steps. The address picks the tax. A model reads the label. And people decide only the exceptions. |
| 0:38 | Front page: three roles | There are three roles: the business owner, a payroll tax expert, and an admin. |
| 0:44 | Business owner: the check | Before anything moves, the owner sees the result. The rules settle three hundred and eleven of three hundred and eighteen items. |
| 0:53 | Business owner: the move | The move takes seconds. Every line is checked against each person's home and work address. |
| 1:00 | Business owner: the summary | Ninety-eight percent is settled by rule. What's left is listed, with its reason and its owner. |
| 1:08 | Business owner: Missing information | Some facts only the employer knows: a home address, where someone works, an account number. One answer each, and the lines finish by themselves. |
| 1:18 | Ask AI | Ask AI speaks first. On any screen, it tells you what matters, then answers your questions. |
| 1:25 | Ask AI on a line | Open a line, and it gives an opinion: what to do, how confident it is, and why. It suggests. People decide. |
| 1:34 | Business owner: nothing left | Nothing is left for the employer. One answer even added a city tax that Desktop never withheld. |
| 1:41 | Payroll tax expert: one decision per label | The expert decides what a label means, once. Each employee's address picks the exact district. |
| 1:49 | Business owner: go live | Four checks pass, and every dollar withheld in Desktop has a destination. Then the confirmed taxes switch on. |
| 1:57 | Admin: the model | And the admin can watch the model work, step by step. |
| 2:03 | The results | For one business: three hundred and eleven items settled by rule, before anyone looks. Every dollar accounted for. Live, and reconciled. |
| 2:14 | Close | Local Tax Migration. Every local tax, checked before it moves. |
