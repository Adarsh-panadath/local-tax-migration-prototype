# Product tour: narration script

The narration of `web/media/tour.mp4`, the film on the front page. It is made for businesses deciding whether to move their payroll: what goes wrong with local taxes, what the product does for them, and what they get. It is spoken by **Kokoro**, the `am_michael` voice (a male American voice), an open-source text-to-speech model (Apache 2.0) run offline, so there is no recording and no paid service. Numbers are written out in words so they are read correctly. Each scene is timed to its sentences; the captions on screen say the same things in fewer words.

Length: 0:57. 107 words, at about 148 words a minute. The figures in the film (98% settled automatically, 5 taxes found that Desktop never withheld, $256,283 traced) are those of the demo business, and the film says so.
